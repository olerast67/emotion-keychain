# Trademarks and the project name

The code, the hardware files and the documentation of this project are open: see [Licences](README.md#licences). The open licences cover copyright (and, for Apache-2.0 and CERN-OHL-W-2.0, patents). **They do not cover the project's identity.** Apache-2.0 says so itself in section 6, and CERN-OHL-W-2.0 says that its Notices include trademark notices which you must keep but which do not grant you rights to the marks.

## What is not licensed

- the project name **"Emotion keychain"** and its Russian name **«Брелок с эмоциями»** when used as the name of a product, kit, shop listing or another project;
- the **OLERAST** brand and its forms (the "OLERAST LINK" boot screen text, the olerast.com domain, the "olerast" manufacturer string);
- the project **logo** and banner artwork;
- the **mascot and the character art style** as a brand: the look of the keychain character in this project's promotional images, packaging and listings.

## What you can do

- **Use, change and sell** the code, the board and the case under their licences: Apache-2.0, CERN-OHL-W-2.0 and CC-BY-4.0. Selling devices is allowed.
- **Name your fork or product differently.** A fork, a derivative board or a device you sell must have its own name and must not use the logo or the OLERAST brand. In builds you distribute, replace "OLERAST" on the boot screen and the bouncing logo (`engine/src/scenes.cpp`) and the manufacturer strings (`firmware/bootloader/emotion_keychain_revb/board.h`, `firmware/boards/keychain_revb.json`).
- **Say where it comes from.** Factual wording is fine and welcome: "compatible with Emotion keychain", "based on the Emotion keychain project", "uses the Emotion keychain face engine". Do not suggest that the original project or its author made, checked or endorses your product.
- **Keep the faces.** The emotion presets in the code and the face renders in the docs are covered by the code and documentation licences. You can ship them; you just cannot use them as your brand in a way that makes buyers think your product is this project.
- **Talk about the project.** Reviews, videos, articles, teaching and personal builds can use the name freely.

If you are unsure, open an issue and ask.

## Что не лицензируется

- бренд **OLERAST** во всех формах (надпись «OLERAST LINK» на заставке, домен olerast.com, строка производителя «olerast»);
- **логотип** проекта и рисунок баннера;
- **маскот и стиль рисовки персонажа** как бренд: облик персонажа брелока на рекламных картинках, упаковке и в карточках товара.

- **Рассказывать о проекте.** Обзоры, видео, статьи, занятия и сборки для себя могут свободно упоминать название.
