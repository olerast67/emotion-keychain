# Emotion Keychain: Owner's Guide

A backpack keychain with a tiny screen and a little character living inside it. It blinks, looks around, gets happy, gets angry, falls asleep in the dark, and reacts to taps, shaking, sound and light. Every keychain has its own personality and looks, for life. It grows, too: over about a month together it unlocks more and more emotions. When it is near other keychains like it, it meets them, makes friends and challenges them to duels.

![The keychain in its case](img/case-3d.png)

## At a glance

| What | How |
|---|---|
| Turn on | short press of the button, or plug in a charger |
| Turn off | hold the button for 3 seconds |
| Duels on/off | double-press the button |
| Sound on/off | triple-press the button |
| Charge | USB-C, a regular 5 V charger or a computer, somewhere warm: 10–42 °C |
| Charging | a bright red light glows on the back |
| Battery life | about 11, 15 or 18 days of normal wear for the S, M and L versions |

## What's where

- **Screen**: on the front, under the clear cover.
- **Button**: on the right edge when you look at the screen.
- **USB-C**: on the bottom edge.
- **Ring loop**: on top.
- **Red charging light**: on the back, seen through the clear base, at the bottom next to the connector.
- **Buzzer hole**: in the base. Don't cover it: when the keychain lies on its back it sounds quieter.

Inside is a lithium-polymer battery. The three versions differ only in the battery, and so in thickness:

| Version | Battery | Normal day | Face on 16 h a day |
|---|---|---|---|
| S | ~150 mAh | ~11 days | ~3 days |
| M | ~200 mAh | ~15 days | ~4 days |
| L | ~250 mAh | ~18 days | ~5 days |

A normal day means 3 hours of face in the light, an hour in dim light, and the rest of the time in a pocket or at night. These are calculated figures; a real keychain may run a little shorter.

## First power-on

1. Press the button on the right edge. You'll see the OLERAST LINK boot screen with a little tune, then the face.
2. Doesn't turn on? Plug in the charger (see below). A plugged-in keychain turns on by itself.
3. Say hello. The character picks its own reactions, and its personality is one of ten: playful, grump, sleepyhead, shy one, curious, chill, drama queen, glitch, trickster, sweetie. About one keychain in ten is rare, and one in a hundred is legendary, with a golden cyber-eye.

## The button

| Press | What happens |
|---|---|
| Short | the character responds: "Boop!", a hello, a wink. If the screen went dark in the dark, it turns back on |
| Double | turns duels on or off. DUELS ON and READY TO HACK on the screen mean on; DUELS OFF and PEACE MODE mean off. On by default |
| Triple | turns sound off or on: SOUND OFF and SILENT MODE mean the keychain is quiet, SOUND ON means it beeps again. The setting is kept even after power-off |
| Hold 3 s | power-off: the character says goodbye right away with GOODNIGHT, even in the middle of a meeting or another scene, and about 7 seconds after you started pressing the screen goes dark |
| Turn on again | short press, or plug in the charger |

- **Presses in a row.** Presses count as a series if the next one starts no later than 0.4 s after you released the previous one; the press itself can be long. With a longer pause, each press counts on its own. Holding for 3 s after a couple of presses only turns the keychain off.
- **When turning on, you can hold the button** "until it turns on": that press won't turn it back off. If the keychain is off while charging, it turns on when you release the button, so just press briefly. There's no need to hold longer than 10 s: that enters the firmware update mode (the KEYCHAIN drive).
- **Don't hold the button while plugging in the cable.** That's a special mode for new firmware: the screen stays dark and the battery doesn't charge. On a wall charger the keychain leaves this mode by itself within 10 seconds. On a computer a KEYCHAIN drive will appear: unplug the cable and plug it in again, this time without touching the button. Don't copy anything to that drive unless the seller sent you new firmware.

## Taps and gestures

- **Tap**: a light knock with your finger on the case. Two taps in a row get a reaction that fits its personality: playful laughs, trickster cackles, sweetie and drama queen fall in love, curious is amazed, chill acts cool, grump gives you a disapproving look, sleepyhead yawns and stretches, shy one gets embarrassed, glitch throws an error. Too many taps in a row get on its nerves: annoyance, anger, and finally a table flip. Once the anger passes, tap it again and it will be embarrassed and apologize.
- **Picked up**: it's happy or wary; if sleepy, it wakes up.
- **Shaking**: surprise; hard and long shaking makes it dizzy, and on public transport it can get motion-sick.
- **Falling**: terror, then a knockout; a third fall within 10 minutes means GAME OVER. Better not to drop it: the case is acrylic.
- **Upside down**: confusion.
- **Sound.** A clap startles it, it listens to talking, and it sways to music.
- **Walking and running**: it walks along with you, and your steps add to its experience.
- **Light and darkness**: see "Night and time" below.

## What the faces mean

There are 139 emotions in total. They are all on the [emotion sheet](img/emotions_sheet.gif) and in the [catalog](emotions.en.md), which explains when each one appears. The main ones that tell you something:

| Face | What it means |
|---|---|
| A battery filling up, a percentage, or CHARGING | the charger was plugged in |
| "Charging" (it's "eating") | charging is in progress; it looks like this while the cable is plugged in, even after it's fully charged |
| "Charged", "Joy", `$_$` | fully charged |
| "Hungry", "Low battery" | 15% charge or less: it's saving power, no surprises |
| "Almost empty" | 5% or less: time to charge |
| GOODNIGHT | turned off with the button |
| LOW BATTERY with a battery icon, then GOODNIGHT | the battery is empty and the keychain turns itself off |
| Asleep, Zzz | it's dark; "Sleepy" means tired or idle for a long time |
| "Cold", "Hot" | the sensor in the processor reads below ~5 °C or above ~38 °C |
| "Bored", "Lonely" | nobody has touched it for a long time |
| NEW in the corner | a new emotion has just been unlocked |

## Charging

1. **Plug USB-C** into a phone charger (5 V), a computer or a power bank. Fast chargers work too: the keychain takes only 5 V from them. Some power banks switch off by themselves when the current is small; in that case, charge from a wall outlet.
2. **A bright red light comes on at the back.** On the screen a battery fills up to the charge percentage, then the character starts "eating".
3. **Charged**: the light no longer glows brightly, it only glows very faintly. The character is happy for a moment. From empty to full takes about 2–3 hours: faster for S, longer for L.

- **The percentage on the screen is an estimate.** The most reliable sign that charging is done is the light. With a weak USB port (an old computer, a long thin cable) the keychain may not notice the end of charging and not show "Charged", although the charging itself will finish correctly.
- **Charges at 10–42 °C** (as read by the sensor in the processor). In the cold the keychain waits until it warms up: the light doesn't glow brightly and there's no charging scene. If it's been out in freezing weather, leave it somewhere warm for 30–60 minutes and charging will start by itself. Above 45 °C charging stops.
- **A keychain that's off turns on when plugged in.** If you want it to charge while off, hold the button for 3 seconds once it's on the charger: the screen goes dark and charging continues. Short press to turn it on.
- **Your computer will see a new USB device** (a serial port). It's a service connection for enthusiasts; you don't need to install anything.
- **On the charger with nothing to do**, after 10 minutes a logo starts bouncing around the screen, like the old DVD screensaver. Sometimes it hits the corner exactly.
- **Don't charge it** under a pillow, on a bed, or unattended overnight. This is standard advice for any lithium battery.

## When the battery runs low

- **15% or less**: "Hungry" and "Low battery", two sad notes. The keychain saves power: fewer frames, no surprises.
- **5% or less**: "Almost empty".
- **Empty**: the keychain says goodbye by itself (LOW BATTERY, GOODNIGHT) and turns off while the battery is still healthy. After that, pressing the button briefly shows an empty battery and turns the keychain off again: the only way to turn it on is to charge it.
- **Charge it within a week.** A keychain that's off still slowly uses up what's left, and after longer than that the battery discharges too deeply and gets damaged.

## Meetings and duels

**Meeting.** Two keychains close together, within 1–3 meters, recognize each other over radio by themselves, with no phone, app or password. Both need to be awake and in the light: in a pocket, in the dark or while asleep a keychain doesn't look for meetings. A meeting usually starts within 1–3 minutes, occasionally up to 5–7: the keychains listen to the radio in short windows to save battery.

- **A new acquaintance**: NEW FRIEND and the neighbor's code, like `K7-3F2A`, with a short vibration. An old one: HI AGAIN.
- **Duels are on by default.** If they're on for the neighbor too, the first meeting of the day with it goes straight to a duel (below) instead of a greeting. The acquaintance still counts, but you won't see NEW FRIEND with the neighbor's code or TWIN!, and the next meeting with it that same day will already be HI AGAIN. To see NEW FRIEND, turn duels off with a double press on at least one of the keychains.
- **With the same neighbor**: no more than once every 10 minutes. If the keychains are together all day (on the same key ring), the breaks get longer: 10, 20, 40, 80, 160 minutes.
- **Rewards for meetings are limited per day:** only the first few meetings of the day give experience.
- **Characters react to each other according to personality:** shy one gets embarrassed, sweetie falls in love, grump rolls its eyes. Matching personalities see TWIN!.

**Duel.** A hacker duel happens if both keychains have duels turned on (double press), once a day with any one keychain. The winner sees WIN! and unlocks a new emotion early; the loser sees LOST and sulks. After the duel the keychains "talk" to each other in a cipher through their buzzers, and at the end ACCESS DENIED flashes on the screen.

**Friendship** grows with the number of days the keychains have met. However many times they meet in a day, it counts as one day.

| Days met | Level |
|---|---|
| 1 | NEW FRIEND: a new acquaintance |
| 3 | BUDDY |
| 7 | FRIENDS |
| 15 | BEST FRIENDS |
| 30 | BFF: friends forever |

Once a day, an older keychain may give a younger one an emotion as a gift (the GIFT scene).

**What the keychain broadcasts over radio:** only its code, level and mood. There's nothing personal in it. But the code never changes, so the keychain is recognizable over radio, just like wireless earbuds.

## Growing up

A new keychain knows 44 emotions. The rest are unlocked with "implants" earned through experience: days together, taps, steps, meetings, duels, charging when it's hungry. With normal wear, everything unlocks in about a month. A new emotion is marked with NEW. There are also 24 achievements with a trophy, such as your first friend, 10,000 steps, or a streak of days together. For now 23 of them can be unlocked on the keychain: the secret knock (KNOCK KNOCK) doesn't work on it yet. Growth and friends are stored in the keychain's memory and aren't lost when it's turned off.

## Night and time

- **The keychain has no battery-backed clock.** It knows it's night by the darkness: in a pocket, in a bag or in a dark room it falls asleep after about ten seconds, and after a minute it turns off the screen to save power. In the light the face comes back in 2–4 seconds, and right away after a tap or a button press.
- **Holidays and easter eggs by date and time** (New Year, your birthday, 13:37 and others) only work if you set the date and time from a computer (see "For enthusiasts" below). After turning off with the button or running out of battery, you need to set them again.

## Storage

- **Putting it away for a long time? Turn it off** (hold the button for 3 s). A keychain left on in a drawer still uses power, about 3 mAh a day; when off, about 10 times less.
- **Store it about half charged** and top it up every 2–3 months.
- **After it shuts off on an empty battery**, charge it within a week.
- Keep it in a dry place at room temperature.

## Care and safety

- **There's a lithium-polymer battery inside.** Don't puncture, bend or squeeze the keychain, and don't take it apart.
- **Don't heat it:** don't leave it in the sun behind a car window, near a radiator, or near fire.
- **It's not waterproof.** Don't get it wet or wash it. If it gets wet, wipe it dry and don't charge it until it has dried out completely, for at least a full day.
- **Use an ordinary USB-C cable.** Cables with a thick jacket sometimes have a thicker plug housing, and such a plug hits the keychain's case and won't go all the way in: use a different cable. Don't put anything into the connector except the plug.
- **If it swells** (the case bulges, the cover or base lifts off, the screen is pushed out from inside): don't charge or use it, and don't puncture it. Put it on a non-flammable surface away from other things, and return it to the seller or take it to a battery collection point.
- **If the case is cracked** so that the battery is visible, don't charge it; contact the seller.
- **Cleaning**: with a soft dry cloth. Alcohol and solvents cause tiny cracks in acrylic.
- **Not for children under 14:** small parts (ring, screws, nuts) and a battery inside. This is not a toy.
- **Disposal**: not with household waste; take it to a battery or electronics collection point.

## Troubleshooting

| What happened | What to do |
|---|---|
| Won't turn on | short press of the button. If that doesn't help, plug in the charger. If an empty battery flashes on the screen for a moment, the battery is empty: charge it |
| The screen went dark by itself | that's by design in the dark: bring it into the light, tap it or press the button |
| Frozen: the face is stuck and it doesn't respond to taps or the button | wait 10 seconds and it will restart by itself. If that doesn't help, hold the button for 3 seconds, then press briefly. If it's charging, unplug the cable and plug it in again |
| Won't charge: the light doesn't glow brightly | warm it up to room temperature (30–60 minutes if it's been out in freezing weather); push the cable all the way in; try a different cable and a different charger |
| Screen is dark on the charger, and a KEYCHAIN drive appears on the computer | the button was held while plugging in, or held for a long time while turning it on during charging: unplug the cable and plug it in again without touching the button |
| Turns itself off, or ignores the button as if it's being held down | the case near the button needs adjusting: contact the seller |
| Doesn't meet other keychains | wait up to 5–7 minutes; both must be in the light, not in a pocket and not asleep; closer than 1–3 meters; if they met this neighbor recently, there's a break of 10 minutes or more. For a duel, both need duels turned on |
| Two new keychains showed DUEL instead of NEW FRIEND | that's by design: duels are on by default, and the first meeting of the day is a duel. The acquaintance still counts |
| No beeping | sound is off: press the button three times in a row (SOUND ON); make sure the buzzer hole in the base isn't covered |
| The face looks dim even though it's bright around | the character is sleepy or asleep, saving power; tap it or press the button. If the time was set from a computer, the screen is also dimmed and the voice is quiet from 23:00 to 6:00 |

## For enthusiasts

The keychain has a USB console. Connect it to a computer and open any serial terminal, such as the Arduino IDE Serial Monitor, at 115200 baud with any line ending except "No Line Ending" ([details](../firmware/release/README.en.md#which-firmware-is-on-the-keychain)). Type a command and press Enter; `?` lists all commands.

- `t 14:30`, `date 2026-12-31`: time and date, for quiet night hours, holidays and easter eggs. After turning off with the button, you need to set them again.
- `bday 05-14`: your birthday. On that day the keychain shows a cake (if the date is set).
- `snd`: sound on/off (same as a triple press); `theme cyber` or `theme retro` for a different theme, `theme classic` for the normal one.
- `ghost`: "invisible mode": the keychain doesn't show itself to others and doesn't meet anyone.
- `me`: who it is, how many steps, friends and duels; `ver`: firmware version.
