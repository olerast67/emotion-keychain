# Boards

KiCad board projects. Every board file — schematic, layout, Gerbers, BOM and CPL — is generated by scripts from a single description (for rev B that is `rev-b/gen/netlist.py`); nothing is edited by hand in KiCad.

| Board | What | Status |
|---|---|---|
| [`rev-b/`](rev-b/README.en.md) | Custom 30 × 46 mm board: nRF52840 module (Raytac MDBT50Q), MCP73832 charger (charging is enabled by the firmware), sensors, buzzer, vibration motor, button, USB-C. A standard SSD1306 0.96″ I2C display module rests on the tabs of the case's acrylic frames, no purchased standoffs. The case is in [`rev-b/case/`](rev-b/case/README.en.md) | Ready to order |
| A | Carrier board for the XIAO nRF52840 Sense with the display on M2 standoffs | Early plan, never built; no folder |
| C | Next revision: fixes from the first boards ([plan](../docs/plan.en.md)) | Planned; no folder yet |

How to order and assemble: see the [rev B board README](rev-b/README.en.md). Prebuilt firmware for prototypes is in [`firmware/release/`](../firmware/release/README.en.md); keychains for sale are flashed with your own build using your own USB IDs (the `[sale]` section in `firmware/platformio.ini`). Components, stack and design decisions: [docs/hardware.en.md](../docs/hardware.en.md).

## Board rules

- FR4 1.6 mm, black solder mask, ENIG finish, rounded corners.
- No traces or exposed copper around the lanyard tab. At least 2.5–3 mm of material between the hole and the board edge.
- USB-C: 2 × 5.1 kΩ resistors on CC, ESD protection on the data lines and a TVS on VBUS.
- The module antenna sits outside the copper, away from the cell and from the tab with the keyring.
- SWD: J3 pads for a Tag-Connect TC2030 cable on the back, no connector. They are only needed for the first flash, before the case is assembled; after that the firmware is updated over USB.
